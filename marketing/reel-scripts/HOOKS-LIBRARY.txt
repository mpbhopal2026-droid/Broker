HOOKS LIBRARY
=============

The first 1.5 seconds decide everything. Instagram counts a view at 3
seconds, so the hook is not an introduction — it is the whole battle.

Swap any of these into any script. Test three hooks on the same body
before writing a new script; the body is rarely the problem.


THE FIVE HOOK TYPES THAT WORK IN FINANCE
========================================

1. AGREE WITH THE SUSPICION
---------------------------
The viewer already distrusts you. Say it first and they stop scrolling
because you took their line away.

  "My dad asked if this is just gambling. Honestly? Often, yes."
  "Our deposits are slower than the big apps. Here is why."
  "You should not trust a trading app you saw on Instagram."
  "Most people who try this lose money. Including people who try us."
  "This is not a get rich video. Scroll on if that is what you wanted."


2. THE COST OF NOT KNOWING
--------------------------
Loss framing outperforms gain framing roughly two to one in finance.

  "Four ways to check if a trading app is real. Takes two minutes."
  "The one thing to check before you send money anywhere."
  "If a platform will not let you withdraw 500 rupees, leave."
  "Check whose name is on the UPI ID. Before you pay, not after."


3. CORRECT A BELIEF
-------------------
People stay to find out if they were wrong.

  "You do not need two lakhs to start. You need zero."
  "Everyone uses a demo account wrong."
  "A demo profit is not proof of anything."
  "Leverage is not a feature. It is a multiplier on being wrong."


4. THE OVERHEARD QUESTION
-------------------------
Feels like advice, not an advert.

  "My friend asked me how you even open a forex account."
  "Someone asked why our verification takes a day."
  "A client asked what happens to his money between UPI and balance."


5. THE FLAT REFUSAL
-------------------
Highest trust. Lowest legal exposure. Nothing to challenge.

  "Here is what we do NOT promise."
  "We do not publish returns. Here is why."
  "No signals. No guarantees. No one will call you."
  "Why should you trust us? Do not. Try the demo first."


HOOKS THAT WILL GET THE ACCOUNT REPORTED
========================================
Do not use these. They are the exact patterns enforcement looks for,
and the exact patterns your audience has been warned about.

  x  "How I turned 10,000 into 40,000"
  x  "Earn daily from your phone"
  x  "This is why the rich do not tell you about forex"
  x  "Comment PROFIT and I will DM you"
  x  Anything with a currency figure in the first frame
  x  Anything using an urgency countdown


MECHANICS
=========
  - Say the hook. Do not introduce it. No "hi guys, welcome back".
  - First frame must be a face or bold text, never a logo.
  - Burn the hook in as text as well — most viewers are on mute.
  - Keep it under 12 words spoken.
  - A pause after the hook line reads as confidence. Use one.

TESTING
=======
Post the same body with three different hooks a week apart. The hook
is nearly always what moved the number, not the script.
