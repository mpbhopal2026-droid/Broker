REEL 17 — "WHY WE ARE SLOWER THAN THEM"
=======================================
LENGTH   24 seconds
PRESENTER Aisha
ANGLE    Behind the scenes. Turns your biggest weakness into the
         reason to trust you.

HOOK (0-3s)
-----------
        "Our deposits are slower than the big apps."
        "I am going to tell you exactly why, and you can decide."

WHY THIS HOOK
-------------
Admitting the weakness in the first three seconds is the whole trick.
The viewer expected a sales pitch and got a confession, so they stay
for the reason.

SHOT LIST
---------
0-3s    HOOK, Aisha direct

3-10s   Screen: deposit sitting pending
        VO: "There is no payment gateway. You pay by UPI, and then a
             person checks your reference number against the bank
             statement before crediting anything."

10-17s  Screen: admin approving (blur any client detail)
        VO: "A gateway would be instant. It would also mean an
             automated system moving money with nobody looking at it."

17-22s  VO: "We chose the slower one. If a deposit is wrong, it gets
             caught by a human before it lands, not after."

22-24s  TEXT: "Slower on purpose."

PRODUCTION NOTE
---------------
Blur every client name, email and amount in the admin footage. Showing
a real client's data in a marketing reel is a DPDP problem, and it is
the kind that ends up screenshotted.
