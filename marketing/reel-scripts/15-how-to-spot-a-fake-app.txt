REEL 15 — "HOW TO SPOT A FAKE TRADING APP"
==========================================
LENGTH   28 seconds
PRESENTER Aisha
ANGLE    Consumer protection. The most shareable script in the folder —
         people send this to family.

HOOK (0-3s)
-----------
        "This app took 40,000 rupees from someone I know."
        "Here is what they missed."

        NOTE: only use this line if it is TRUE of someone the client
        actually knows. If not, use the alternate:
        "Four ways to check if a trading app is real. Takes 2 minutes."

SHOT LIST
---------
0-3s    HOOK, direct to camera

3-9s    TEXT: "1. Can you withdraw a small amount?"
        VO:   "Deposit the minimum. Withdraw it the next day. If that
               is difficult, you have learned everything you need to
               for a small price."

9-15s   TEXT: "2. Whose name is on the UPI ID?"
        VO:   "If money goes to a personal account with an unrelated
               name, ask why. Ask before you pay, not after."

15-21s  TEXT: "3. Is there a written risk warning?"
        VO:   "Every legitimate platform is required to say most
               people lose. If nobody has told you that, they are
               hiding it deliberately."

21-26s  TEXT: "4. Do they contact you first?"
        VO:   "Real platforms do not cold-call, do not DM you a
               signal group, and do not have a manager who guarantees
               returns."

26-28s  VO: "Check us with all four. We will wait."

WHY THIS WORKS
--------------
It is genuinely useful to a viewer who never becomes a customer, which
is what makes people share it. And every test is one the platform
passes — you are being audited on your own terms.
