PRESENTER PERSONA — "AISHA"
===========================

Use the SAME seed / character across every presenter reel. A recurring
face builds recognition on Reels faster than any logo does.

CHARACTER
---------
  Indian woman, late 20s
  Shoulder-length dark hair, minimal makeup
  Smart-casual: plain kurta, or blazer over a plain tee
  Neutral home-office or quiet cafe background, soft daylight
  Warm and unhurried. Speaks like she is explaining to a friend.
  NOT high-energy hype delivery.

AI GENERATION PROMPT (HeyGen / Synthesia / Kling)
-------------------------------------------------
Indian woman, late 20s, shoulder-length dark hair, minimal makeup,
plain navy kurta. Seated, soft natural window light, neutral
home-office background, shallow depth of field. Direct eye contact
with camera. Calm conversational delivery, slight smile, natural
hand gestures. Vertical 1080x1920, medium close-up, chest up.

VOICE DIRECTION
---------------
  Pace: unhurried, roughly 150 words per minute
  Tone: explaining, not selling
  No rising "influencer" inflection at line ends
  Pauses are allowed. They read as confidence.

WHY SHE NEVER CLAIMS RESULTS
----------------------------
She is a brand host, not a customer. She explains, asks and answers.
The moment an AI face says "I made money on this", it becomes a
fabricated testimonial. See 00-RULES-READ-FIRST.txt.
