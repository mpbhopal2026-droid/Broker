WHY THIS ORDER
--------------
The first three posts set the account's character before any of them
ask for anything. In a feed where every forex account opens with a
promise, opening with "here is what we don't promise" is the whole
positioning. Product-led reels convert far better once that groundwork
exists.

Reel 11 (full walkthrough) and Reel 12 (risk talk) belong pinned to
the profile permanently. They are what a cautious person watches
before deciding, and cautious people make the best clients.

WHAT TO MEASURE
---------------
Not likes. Watch:
  - Demo account signups     the real conversion event
  - Watch-through past 50%   whether the honesty angle holds attention
  - Saves and shares         trust signal, beats likes in this category
  - Comments asking a real question, not "DM for signals"

WHAT TO DO IF SOMEONE COMMENTS ASKING FOR RETURNS
-------------------------------------------------
Answer publicly, once, plainly: "We don't publish return figures and
we don't offer signals." Do not delete the comment. The public answer
is worth more than the comment costs you.
