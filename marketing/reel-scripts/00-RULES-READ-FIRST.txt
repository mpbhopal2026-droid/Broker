RULES — READ BEFORE PUBLISHING
==============================

NEVER PUT IN A REEL
-------------------
  x  Any number that looks like a return
       "10,000 -> 40,000", profit screenshots, P&L figures
  x  A named person claiming they made money
       That is a testimonial. Unlicensed, it is the highest-risk
       element you could publish.
  x  The words: guaranteed / risk-free / assured / secure returns
       Regulator keyword-matching finds these first.
  x  Lifestyle content — cars, cash, watches, "financial freedom"
  x  Any claim of live trading. It is not enabled yet.

ALWAYS INCLUDE
--------------
  +  Burned-in risk line, visible for the FULL duration, bottom third:
       "Leveraged trading carries a high risk of loss.
        Most retail accounts lose money."
     Small text. Costs nothing. It is the clearest single signal that
     you are not a scam.
  +  Disclosure that the presenter is AI-generated (bio or on-screen
     tag). Meta's synthetic-media rules are tightening and undisclosed
     AI faces in financial ads are what enforcement targets.

THE TESTIMONIAL LINE
--------------------
An AI presenter as an obvious brand host is normal advertising.
An AI presenter saying "I made money on this app" is a fabricated
testimonial — deceptive, and the single riskiest thing you could
publish without a licence.

Every script here has her EXPLAINING or ASKING. Never claiming
personal results. Keep it that way if you write more.

FOOTAGE
-------
Do NOT let AI generate the app screens. Record the real app on a real
phone. AI-generated trading UI invents impossible numbers and broken
charts, and on a finance advert that one detail is what makes a viewer
decide it is a scam. The real UI is good. Show it.

FORMAT
------
  1080 x 1920 vertical
  Hook inside the first 1.5 seconds
  Burned-in captions — around 85% watch on mute
  15-22 seconds is the sweet spot
  Calm, factual voice. Every hype-energy forex reel sounds like the
  scams people have been warned about. Sounding boring is a
  competitive advantage in this category.
