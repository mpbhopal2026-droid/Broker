REEL 18 — "WHAT A DEMO ACCOUNT IS ACTUALLY FOR"
===============================================
LENGTH   25 seconds
PRESENTER Aisha
ANGLE    Reframe. Most people misuse demo accounts and then blame
         themselves.

HOOK (0-3s)
-----------
        "Everyone uses a demo account wrong."
        "It is not there to prove you can win."

SHOT LIST
---------
0-3s    HOOK

3-10s   Screen: demo terminal, badge visible
        VO: "Demo money is not real, so you take risks you never would
             with your own. Then the profit means nothing and the
             confidence is borrowed."

10-17s  TEXT: "Use it to learn the BUTTONS, not the market"
        VO:   "Where is the stop loss. What does the spread do to
               your entry. How long does a withdrawal take. Learn the
               machine, not the market."

17-22s  VO: "Trade it as if it were your own money, or the practice
             teaches you nothing."

22-25s  TEXT: "Free practice account. globalforex.online"

WHY THIS ONE MATTERS
--------------------
It is the only script here that gives advice a viewer can act on
tonight, and it costs you nothing to say. It also quietly sets a
realistic expectation before they ever fund an account — which is
worth more in reduced complaints than it costs in signups.
