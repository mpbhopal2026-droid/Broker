REEL 02 — "WHERE DID MY MONEY GO?"
==================================
LENGTH   22 seconds
ANGLE    Deposit transparency — your genuinely strongest feature
STYLE    Screen recording led

SHOT LIST
---------
0-3s   Split screen: a confusing payment gateway vs your UPI QR
       TEXT: "Most platforms hide where your money goes."

3-9s   Screen recording: deposit page, QR appears, amount fills in
       TEXT: "Scan. Pay. Same UPI ID shown on screen."
       VO:   "The QR is built from the UPI ID printed under it.
              They can never disagree."

9-15s  UTR field, submit
       TEXT: "Enter your UTR."
       VO:   "We match it against the bank statement by hand."

15-20s Ledger view
       TEXT: "Every rupee, on a statement you can see."

20-22s Logo
       TEXT: "Transparent by design"

WHY THIS ONE WORKS
------------------
The QR being generated from the same UPI ID shown as text is a real
engineering decision most competitors fake with a static uploaded
image. Lead with what is actually true and verifiable on screen.

CAPTION
-------
Manual matching. Slower than a gateway. You can see every step.

*Leveraged trading carries a high risk of loss.*
