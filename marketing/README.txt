BEFORE YOU PUBLISH ANY OF THESE
-------------------------------
Read 00-RULES-READ-FIRST.txt. It is short and it is the difference
between an advert and a regulatory problem.

Two constraints shaped every script:

1. Live trading is DISABLED in the app (no licensed market-data feed).
   No script may promise live execution. All of them lead with the demo
   account and account opening instead.

2. Advertising forex to Indian retail is where the missing licence gets
   loud. RBI publishes an Alert List of unauthorised forex platforms, and
   advertising is how platforms get named on it. Every script avoids
   profit claims, income screenshots and lifestyle content — not only
   because it is safer, but because those are the exact signals that get
   an account banned and an audience suspicious.
